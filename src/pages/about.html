About

NoByte is a collection of small, free tools that run entirely in your browser. It exists for one reason: the everyday jobs people search for online, such as formatting JSON or turning an image into a PDF, should not require an account or an upload, and should not come wrapped in advertising.

Every tool does its work on your own device. The text and files you use are never sent to a server, so there is nothing for anyone to collect or leak. The single exception is the DNS lookup, which needs a resolver in order to function. The privacy page sets out exactly what it sends.

The site has no accounts and no analytics. Because it is built as a set of static pages, each tool continues to work once loaded, even without a connection.
