Privacy

Last updated {{year}}.

NoByte has no accounts and asks you for nothing. What you paste, type, or drop into a tool is not collected, because it never leaves your browser in the first place.

What stays on your device

Almost every tool processes your input entirely within your browser. Anything you paste or open remains on your own device and is never uploaded. This applies to every tool except the two described below.

The two tools that reach the network

The DNS lookup sends the domain name you enter to a public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (Google, with Cloudflare as a fallback) and displays the response. The dictionary sends the single word you look up to dictionaryapi.dev. Neither sends anything further. Neither tool can work without asking someone, which is why they are the only exceptions. Both services operate under their own privacy policies, and NoByte gives them no more than the domain or the word needed to answer.

What is measured

Basic visit statistics are collected, so there is some idea of which tools people use. No cookie is set for this, and no profile is built.

What is never measured is the part that matters most: nothing you enter into a tool is recorded, sent, or seen.

What your browser stores

Some tools save a preference or your recent input in the browser's local storage so that they resume where you left off. This information remains in your browser, and clearing your site data removes it.
