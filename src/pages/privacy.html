Privacy

Last updated {{year}}.

NoByte does not use accounts and does not collect personal information. No analytics or advertising scripts run on the site.

What stays on your device

Almost every tool processes your input entirely within your browser. Anything you paste or open remains on your own device and is never uploaded. This applies to every tool except the one described below.

The one tool that reaches the network

The DNS lookup sends the domain name you enter to a public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (Google, with Cloudflare as a fallback) and displays the response. It sends nothing further. A lookup cannot function without querying a resolver, which is why it is the only exception. The resolver operates under its own privacy policy, and NoByte provides it with no more than the domain needed to answer the query.

What your browser stores

Some tools save a preference or your recent input in the browser's local storage so that they resume where you left off. This information remains in your browser, and clearing your site data removes it.
