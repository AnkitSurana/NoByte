Converted amounts

Rates are the European Central Bank's daily reference rates, served by Frankfurter. They are fixed once a business day — a daily reference, not a live tick-by-tick feed — and the date they apply to is shown under the results. Fetched rates are cached in your browser, so the converter keeps working offline. Only currency codes are sent to the service; the amount you convert never leaves your device.
