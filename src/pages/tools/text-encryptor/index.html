Put a message in the box and press Lock to encrypt it, or paste an encrypted block and press Unlock to read it. The result flips into view below, ready to copy. Encryption is AES-GCM with a key from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 200,000 rounds); nothing is uploaded and the passphrase is never stored, so a lost passphrase means a lost message.
