How to play

Race the clock. Each word is 3 to 6 letters and the grid shrinks to fit, greying the spare cells. Solve one for +time, miss one and lose time. Stuck? Hint gives you one letter in its right place for a few seconds off the clock, twice per word. Run out of rows or run out of time and the word is spelled out for you. Beginner is shorter words and a longer clock; Advanced is longer words and less time.
