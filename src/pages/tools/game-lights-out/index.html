How to play

Pressing a cell flips it and the four beside it. Turn every light off. Only which cells you press matters, not the order, and pressing the same cell twice undoes it.

The usual method is to work downwards. Ignore the top row for a moment: for every light still on in row one, press the cell directly below it, which turns that light off. Repeat row by row and the lights all get pushed to the bottom row, which then tells you which cells in the top row to press to finish. Hint marks a cell on a shortest path to clear and tells you how many presses are left, so you can also just follow it.
