How to play

Pick a square to place your X. Three in a row wins. Tic-tac-toe is a solved game: two players who never err always draw, so the opponent has three settings. Fair plays the best move about three times in four and settles for second best the rest of the time, which is where your openings come from. Easy takes a win and blocks yours, and is otherwise picking at random. Perfect never errs, and a draw is the best you can get.
