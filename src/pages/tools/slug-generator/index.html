{{tool-hero:slug-generator}}

A slug is the readable tail of a URL, like /blog/how-to-bake-sourdough. Paste a post title, product name, or heading to get a clean slug for your CMS or router, instead of typing one by hand. It all runs in your browser.

  • Accented letters fold to ASCII, so café becomes cafe.
  • Characters outside the Latin alphabet, such as CJK, are dropped.
  • Strip stop words drops filler like "the", "and", and "of".
