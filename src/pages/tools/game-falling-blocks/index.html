How to play

Fill a full row to clear it and score; don't let the stack reach the top. The faint shadow shows where the piece will land. ←→ move, ↑ rotate, ↓ drop, Space hard-drop, P pause. On a phone: use the keys below the board, holding left, right, or down to repeat. You can also tap the board to rotate, drag sideways to move, and swipe down to drop.
