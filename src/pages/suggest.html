Got a tool in mind?

If there's something you keep wishing this site had, name it. Good ones get built. No account, no form firing off to a server. The button just opens your own email with everything filled in, so nothing leaves your device until you hit send. Same as every tool here.

no account no sign-up

The tools that fit best are ones your browser can do alone, like converting a file or cleaning up text. Ones that need a login or the live internet aren't a fit for us.

Open in email

If your mail app didn't open, tap Copy it instead and paste the text into any email addressed to hello@nobyte.in.

This all happens in your browser. NoByte keeps no inbox on its servers, so your idea lands straight in a personal one. Nothing you type here is stored or counted.
