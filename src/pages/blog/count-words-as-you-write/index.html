Knowing how long your text is, as you write it

Word and character limits are everywhere, and guessing at them by eye rarely works. Here is a quick way to know where you stand while you are still writing.

Words and characters both matter

Some limits count words, like an essay or an article, and some count characters, like a social post or a meta description. The two are not interchangeable, so it helps to see both at once rather than converting one into the other in your head.

Reading time is an estimate

A reading-time figure is worked out from the word count at an average pace, so treat it as a rough guide rather than a promise. It is handy for pacing a talk or a blog post, and less useful for anything that has to be exact.

Count as you go

Rather than writing first and trimming afterwards, keep the count in view while you draft. Paste or type into the word counter and the totals update on every keystroke, so you can settle on a limit instead of overshooting and cutting back.
